[h1]Morning Call[/h1]

A lightweight multiplayer sleep helper for Project Zomboid Build 42.21.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Shows who is currently sleeping, for example: [code]Sleeping 1/3: Alice[/code]
[*]Wakes sleeping online players at 05:30 every in-game day
[*]Plays a rooster crow and displays a six-second morning message
[*]Leaves fatigue, sleep duration, and time acceleration to the vanilla game
[*]Designed to coexist with MP Fast Forward
[/list]

No sandbox options, keybinds, dependencies, or saved data.
